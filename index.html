Play safely

18+ For adults only. You must be 18 or over to play the National Lottery.

A fair warning: every ball has the same chance in every draw, so sticking to the most-drawn numbers or combinations doesn't improve your odds. The differences in the counts are ordinary random variation. It's a fun way to choose, not a system. Only spend what you can afford.

Need to talk? If gambling stops being fun, the free, confidential National Gambling Helpline is open 24 hours a day on 0808 8020 133, or chat online at gamcare.org.uk. More advice at begambleaware.org.
